# Code of Conduct

RPS Arena is committed to a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient in technical discussions.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Use inclusive language and assume good intent while still addressing harmful behavior.
- Respect privacy; do not post another person's private information.
- Keep reports, reviews, and discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate intimidation, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, or repeated disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, lock, or reject content that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

For conduct concerns, contact `supportramsandesh@gmail.com`. For security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of posting details publicly.
