## What to include in a bug report

Provide:

- RPS Arena version;
- Android/Desktop platform and OS version;
- JDK/Gradle versions for build problems;
- exact reproduction steps;
- expected behavior;
- actual behavior;
- sanitized logs or screenshots when useful;
- whether a round timer, backup import, language, or private-room reference flow was involved.

Do not include account passwords, access tokens, signing keys, private certificates, or another person's private data.
